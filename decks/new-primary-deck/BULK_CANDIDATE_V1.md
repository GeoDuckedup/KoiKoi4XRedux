## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished 5:8 portrait card-face illustration for a mobile
Hanafuda-inspired game. Use the approved contemporary Japanese woodblock and screen-print fusion:
deep indigo-to-charcoal full-bleed ground, mineral-gold linework and flecks, restrained vermilion,
muted teal, ivory, and seasonal botanical accents, subtle tactile washi grain, crisp layered
silhouettes, elegant negative space, and large mobile-readable shapes. Keep the primary subject in
the centered ART_SPEC v1 84% × 88% safe area while backgrounds bleed. Create original artwork only.
Do not include text, letters, numbers, calligraphy, labels, signatures, logos, watermarks, UI,
category badges, borders, rounded corners, frames, card outlines, photorealism, anime characters, or
commercial-deck imitation.

## Owner-directed revision

On August 9, 2026, the owner rejected the first `july-bush-clover-plain-b` output because it depicted
the wrong flower. The replacement used the original card as the edit target and July Plain A as a
supporting botanical/style reference. The final edit prompt required botanically recognizable
Japanese bush clover / hagi (Lespedeza): long slender arching woody sprays, many small purple-magenta
pea-shaped flowers along the sprays, and small oval trifoliate leaflets. It explicitly prohibited
lawn clover, chrysanthemums, pom-poms, and large composite flower heads while preserving the airy
upper-left branch, open indigo center, low lower-right hillside, approved palette, washi texture, and
plain-card visual weight. The rejected native was
`exec-86dedd7c-bec5-42ad-94af-a700626e357e.png`; it is not mapped by `deck.json`.
