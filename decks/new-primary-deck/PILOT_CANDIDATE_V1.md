## Shared direction

- contemporary Japanese woodblock and screen-print fusion;
- deep indigo and charcoal grounds;
- mineral gold, vermilion, teal, ivory, and restrained seasonal accents;
- bold mobile-readable silhouettes with subtle washi-paper grain;
- full-bleed illustration with no baked-in border, label, text, logo, UI, or watermark;
- critical subjects kept within the ART_SPEC v1 84% × 88% safe area;
- original premium tabletop-game imagery rather than imitation of a commercial deck.

## Prompt set

### `december-phoenix` — Bright / large focal

Create a strict 5:8 full-bleed portrait of a majestic mineral-gold phoenix rising around stylized
paulownia blossoms at winter dusk. Use a charcoal/plum ground, vermilion and amber highlights,
small teal accents, broad woodblock/screen-print shapes, and a single unmistakable mobile-readable
silhouette. Keep the phoenix centered in the safe area; allow only decorative foliage to bleed.

### `september-sake-cup` — simple composition

Using the Phoenix medium and palette discipline as a style reference only, create one warm-ivory
ceramic sake cup with visible vermilion sake, framed by a restrained lower arc of mineral-gold
chrysanthemums and muted teal leaves. Preserve generous negative space and make the cup the one
unmistakable symbol at mobile size.

### `january-pine-plain-a` — representative Plain

Using the established medium as a style reference only, create a quiet windswept black-pine bough
rising diagonally from the lower left. Use a few broad dark-green needle masses, textured bark,
restrained ivory snow, open upper-right negative space, and sparse gold flecks. Avoid Bright, Animal,
or Scroll symbols.

### `november-rain` — dense composition

Using the established medium as a style reference only, create a compact traveler beneath one large
vermilion oil-paper umbrella, a wind-bent willow, diagonal pale-blue rain, a bright rippling stream,
and one small frog. Keep the umbrella and willow immediately legible despite the layered weather and
landscape detail.

### Card back

Create a strict 5:8 full-bleed, 180-degree rotationally symmetric back: four abstract koi-like curves
interlock around a compact gold chrysanthemum rosette, surrounded by broad seigaiha-inspired waves.
Use deep indigo-black, muted teal, vermilion, mineral gold, and small ivory accents; include no text,
identity marker, border, or directional cue.
